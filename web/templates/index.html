How the bill works

The system applies the parking tariff according to the time spent in the parking area. The parking charge is then used as the subtotal. {% if vat_enabled %}VAT = {{"%.0f"|format(vat_rate * 100)}}% of the parking charge, and Total due = parking charge + VAT.{% else %}VAT is currently disabled in configuration.{% endif %}

Example: a KSh 50 parking charge has KSh 8 VAT, making the total payable KSh 58.
